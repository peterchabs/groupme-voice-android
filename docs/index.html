Sign in your watch

VoiceMe signs in on your Android phone with GroupMe's own sign-in, then sends it to your watch.

1 The code on your watch

If you scanned the code on your watch, this is already filled in.

2 Get the VoiceMe phone app

Already have it? Skip to step 3.

Download for Android

Open the downloaded file to install it. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser first.

3 Sign in and send it to your watch

Sign in with GroupMe the way you always do (Google, Microsoft, Apple, email or phone), then tap Send to watch. Your watch says hi to you by name a few seconds later.

Needs an Android phone. VoiceMe's sign-in runs in its phone app, which isn't available for iPhone.

Not affiliated with GroupMe or Microsoft. VoiceMe is an independent, open-source project. Your sign-in goes from the phone app to your watch locked with the watch's code, so only that watch can open it. How it works
